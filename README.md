# Kader Client Hub

Official client hub for Kader services, portfolio, WhatsApp contact, and smart menu offers.

## About Kader

Kader is a creative advertising service focused on helping restaurants, cafés, sweets shops, schools, and small businesses present their products and services with clearer commercial value.

Kader does not sell beautiful design only.  
Kader helps turn products into clear advertisements that make customers understand the offer, feel the value, and move closer to ordering.

## Main Services

### 1. Product Advertisement
A clear product ad for restaurants, cafés, sweets, drinks, and local businesses.

Includes:
- Product idea
- Visual direction
- Marketing caption
- Story version
- CTA

### 2. Kader Smart Menu
A smart digital menu for restaurants and cafés.

Includes:
- Mobile-friendly menu
- Fixed link
- QR code
- Categories
- Contact and WhatsApp button
- Easy price update system

### 3. School Content
Professional content for schools and educational activities.

Includes:
- School announcements
- Event designs
- Certificates
- Presentations
- Short videos

## Business Goal

The goal of this hub is to make Kader easier to understand for clients and help them choose the right service quickly.
